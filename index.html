Privacy Policy — Tilefall

Last updated: 2026-08-07

This policy explains how the mobile game Tilefall ("the App") handles information. The App is developed and published by an individual developer (contact below).

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The App has no account system, no login, and no server of our own. We do not collect, transmit, or store your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or files.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device only (cleared stages, your best time per stage, selected language, sound on/off). This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data from Android settings.

3. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system version, and interactions with ads. This processing is performed by Google, not by us.

  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can limit ad personalization at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device, where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID.

4. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are located in a region where consent is required, the App shows Google's consent form (UMP SDK) when it starts, and your choice is passed to Google. You can change your choice later by clearing the App's data.

5. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell or share your personal information. The only third party involved is Google, as described in section 3.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: rudy00candy@gmail.com

  • Googleプライバシーポリシー：https://policies.google.com/privacy

広告のパーソナライズは、Android端末の 設定 → Google → 広告 からいつでも制限でき、 広告IDのリセットや削除も同じ画面から行えます。

同意の取得が必要な地域では、起動時にGoogleの同意フォーム（UMP SDK）を表示し、選択結果をGoogleへ渡します。 選択をやり直したい場合は、アプリのデータを削除してください。
